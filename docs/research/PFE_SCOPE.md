# PFE Scope

## PFE objective

Build and evaluate a prototype of DBSARE as an AI-assisted cybersecurity investigation and defense architecture.

## PFE boundary

The PFE should demonstrate a coherent vertical slice rather than claim completion of every long-term subsystem.

## Demonstration targets

- Controlled cybersecurity environment
- Evidence collection
- Normalization
- Environment representation
- Brain/relationship concepts
- Reasoning
- Investigation workflow
- Permission-controlled actions where safely implemented
- Audit/reporting

Future research extends beyond the PFE prototype.
